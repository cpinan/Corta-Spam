Permission explainer Android

Shown before the OS default-dialer dialog. Plain language on why the app needs this, plus an explicit "what we'll never do" reassurance.

Explainer screen
9:41

Set as default phone app

We need to become your default phone app

This is the only way Android lets an app see who's calling before you answer, and speak a greeting to callers you haven't approved yet.

Screen calls silently. We check the number against your rules before your phone ever rings.

Power the optional auto-responder. Only used if you turn it on yourself in Settings.

You can switch back to your phone's original dialer at any time in Android Settings.

What we will never do
Record any call unless you separately turn that on, per call type.
Send your call data, contacts, or numbers off this device.
Show ads or share information with third parties.
This app is open source — view the code
Then Android shows its own dialog
System UI, not ours

Allow BloqueaLlamadas to be your default phone app?

This app will handle your calls and caller ID.

DENYALLOW

This native dialog is controlled by Android, not by us — our explainer only prepares you for it.

On iOS
Not available on iOS

iOS doesn't allow a default-dialer role

Apple doesn't let third-party apps intercept or answer calls before the system does, so this screen doesn't exist on iOS.

Instead, BloqueaLlamadas registers as a Call Directory extension: it labels and silently blocks known spam numbers using Apple's own "Silence unknown callers" system.

Auto-responder script editor Android

Greeting script with live TTS preview, and a deliberately alarming toggle for recording that injects a non-removable consent line. Try the toggle below — it's live.

Editor interactive
9:41

Auto-responder

Greeting script

124/220

Record caller's message

Off by default. Turning this on records audio after the greeting and adds a consent notice the caller hears — it can't be removed from the script.

Locked into the script: "This call may be recorded. By continuing, you consent to being recorded." Callers always hear this before recording starts.

Full preview

Hi, you've reached a call screened by BloqueaLlamadas. Please say your name and reason for calling after the tone. This call may be recorded. By continuing, you consent to being recorded.
On iOS
Not available on iOS

iOS doesn't let apps answer calls

Apple's CallKit doesn't allow any third-party app to pick up a call, play audio, or record on your behalf — so there's no auto-responder screen on iOS.

The closest built-in equivalent is Apple's own "Silence unknown callers," which BloqueaLlamadas can label numbers for but not replace.

This is a platform limit, not a choice we made.

Tablet & landscape layouts

Same Compose Multiplatform screens, reflowed at wider window sizes. Bottom navigation becomes a navigation rail once there's room for it, and list screens gain a persistent detail pane instead of a bottom sheet once there's room for two panes side by side.

Compact · <600dp wide

Phone, portrait

Bottom navigation bar, single pane, bottom sheets for detail. Covered in screens 1–7 above.

Medium · 600–839dp wide

Small tablet, or phone rotated

Navigation rail replaces the bottom bar. Still one pane — content just gets wider margins and denser grids.

Expanded · ≥840dp wide

Large tablet landscape

Navigation rail widens into a labeled drawer. List screens split into list + persistent detail pane.
